This release focuses on making the topology easier to read — switch rings now draw as actual loops, links are coloured by speed, and you can trace a path hop by hop. Issues also got smarter about surfacing scans and daemons that need attention.

## New Features

- Draw rings of switches as loops in the Layer 2 view, with ports and spurs arranged so ring links no longer cross each other.
- Colour links by speed in both the Layer 2 and simplified views, and show physical links on simplified maps so you can see the real cabling at a glance.
- Trace a path link by link from the edge inspector to follow traffic across your network.
- Surface failed scans and silent daemons as Issues, and export any Issues list to CSV.
- Edit subnet ranges and VLAN links by hand, and record what each new subnet is for when you create it.
- Add a Water Utility demo site built on two fiber rings, showing off the new ring layout with realistic port-to-port links.

## Improvements

- Add a Display toggle that controls whether container names appear when you zoom out, keeping whatever you have selected in full detail.
- Speed up the first draw of large topologies and stop the topology list from reloading every time you switch sites.
- Ask what brings you to Scanopy during signup and recommend a plan based on your answer.
- Scan a daemon's own networks alongside any routed scan targets, and kick off the scan straight from Create and scan.
- Offer to merge duplicate hosts when LLDP neighbours are ambiguous, and absorb inferred duplicates automatically.
- Add guided troubleshooting for an individual daemon's connection, including detection of daemons running inside WSL2.
- Link out to the relevant documentation from warnings, the setup wizard and Issues.
- Rename unmatched ports to Unclaimed and tighten vendor matching so fewer devices are mislabelled.

## Bug Fixes

- Fix the topology failing to load when its tab is opened after the page has already settled.
- Keep the viewport steady when selecting a node opens the details panel.
- Close open popovers when you click on the topology canvas.
- Ignore 0.0.0.0/0 interface subnets so they no longer appear as scan targets.
- Require and default a site in the daemon wizard, preventing daemons from being created without one.
- Keep the loading bar visible while a scan re-run is pending.
